## How they were made

- Tool: VOICEVOX ENGINE 0.25.2 (CPU build, official Docker image
  `voicevox/voicevox_engine:cpu-amd64-ubuntu22.04-latest`), run locally.
  https://voicevox.hiroshiba.jp/
- Speaker: 青山龍星 (voice library by ななはぴ / VirVoX Project).
- Per-line tuning in the audio query: lower pitchScale (-0.08 to -0.11),
  intonationScale 1.4 to 1.7, stretched held vowels (e.g. ストラ「ー」イク),
  raised volumeScale.
- Post-processing with ffmpeg: slight pitch-down (rubberband, x0.94, formants
  preserved), EQ (+4 dB @150 Hz, +4.5 dB @3 kHz, -2 dB @400 Hz), compression,
  tanh soft-clip saturation, short stadium slapback echo (75/160 ms), limiter,
  peak-normalised to about -1 dBFS. Encoded as mono MP3 96 kbps and Ogg Vorbis.

No game, broadcast or other third-party recordings were used.
